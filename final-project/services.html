Services

Small animal services

  • Wellness visits – Routine wellness exams are important to help keep your pets healthy and can detect problems before they become more serious. These can include dental disease, heart problems, lumps and bumps, and much more
  • Vaccinations – Vaccines are an important part of keeping your pet healthy. We tailor our vaccine recommendations to each individual based on their lifestyle and potential risks they are exposed to.
  • Sick visits – When your pet isn’t feeling well, we’re here to help diagnose their problems as well as recommend the best treatment options to help get them back on their feet
  • Diagnostics – We have a full-range of in-house diagnostics available including bloodwork, urinalysis, fecal examinations, digital x-ray, and ultrasound.
  • Surgical services – We perform a wide-range of surgical services including routine spays and neuters as well as more advanced procedures such as lump removals, GI surgery, bladder stone removal, and more.
  • Dentistry – Dental disease is one of the most common findings in our pets. Dental cleanings as well as extractions when necessary are an important part of keeping your pet healthy.

Large animal services

  • Herd Health – We can help you establish appropriate vaccination and parasite control protocols to keep your herd healthy
  • Pregnancy Checking – We utilize ultrasound for pregnancy checking to help provide the most accurate diagnostics and staging
  • Green tag program – Green tagging feeder calves can help increase the value of your calf crop. We can help you prepare your calves for market.
  • Reproduction Services – Bovine ET & AI can be a great tool to increase the genetic potential of your herd. We can provide these services as well as helping with synchronization protocols to make your breeding season more efficient.
  • OB and Neonatal Care – Calving, lambing, and kidding can be a stressful time. We can help if you’re having problems whether it’s difficult birthing, sick babies, or prolapses.

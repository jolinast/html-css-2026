About us

Mount Vernon Veterinary Clinic was established in 2021 by Dr. Rik Smith. We are independently owned and operated and provide veterinary care to both small and large animal patients in the Mount Vernon and Lisbon communities as well as the surrounding area.

Meet the team

Veterinarians

  • Dr. Rik Smith: Dr. Smith is a native of Nebraska and came to Iowa for vet school. He completed his undergraduate work at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and is a 2009 graduate of Iowa State University College of Veterinary Medicine. He worked as an associate veterinarian in Eldridge and Anamosa before starting Mount Vernon Veterinary Clinic in 2021, fulfilling his dream of practice ownership. He enjoys all aspects of being a mixed animal veterinarian with a particular interest in surgery, bovine reproduction, and livestock herd work. When not at work, he enjoys spending time with his wife and 3 kids. He enjoys sports, woodworking, and all types of outdoor activities. The Smith family has a dog named Copper as well as a rotating menagerie of animals his kids bring home.
